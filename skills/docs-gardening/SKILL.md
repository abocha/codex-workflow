# Documentation gardening

Make the scoped task route support correct decisions. Mechanical green, age, link counts and
wording polish do not establish semantic correctness. Local governance owns lifecycle policy,
verification dates, required checks and generated inventories.

Choose the relevant entry question:

- A change retires a concept, moves ownership or finishes a plan: follow the changed behavior,
  its documented claims and known consumers. Repair routes in the same change.
- A named claim or route is suspect: walk a representative task and inspect the actual surface
  the prose describes. Give evidence room to support KEEP.
- Requested maintenance: use still-applicable reports as leads; generate a fresh snapshot only
  when it answers the question. Age/overlap/no-link heuristics are not archival verdicts.

Read [semantic review and disposition](references/semantic-review.md) for the investigation.
Prefer the corrected owner, repaired consumers and a concise task explanation over a new ledger.
Read-only requests remain read-only; the skill adds no tracker, provider, production or data authority.

Apply project-specific docs checks and freshness-date requirements. An audit of selected claims
does not establish full-document verification. Preserve historical observations/provenance and
move still-useful procedures before archiving completed execution context.

Finish when the scoped route supports sound decisions, material contradictions are resolved,
unique constraints survive, required evidence exists and remaining consequential uncertainty
has an owner. Apply [evidence and closure](../codex-workflow/references/evidence.md) rather than
continuing cosmetic refinement or broad archaeology.
