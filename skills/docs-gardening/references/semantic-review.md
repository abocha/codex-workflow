# Semantic review and disposition

Frame the investigation around a task and the decision its documentation must support. Inspect
the exact implementation surface: runtime default, public response, internal projection, emitted
metric and deployed observation can describe different things. A name match or neighboring test
does not prove the claim. Source establishes implementation, not necessarily intended behavior
or production activation. Resolve disagreements with binding contracts before deciding whether
code, prose or unresolved intent is the defect.

For each material finding, locate the claim/route, evidence, plausible task consequence and surviving
owner. Do not invent a stronger assertion to refute. Accurate summaries remain useful even when
source has more detail; extra caveats should change a needed decision.

- KEEP when evidence supports the guidance, intent or historical role.
- CORRECT a contradicted fact or consequential ambiguity at its owner.
- CONSOLIDATE competing guidance after choosing the survivor and transferring unique current material.
- ARCHIVE completed/superseded context after preserving useful guidance and repairing consumers.
- REMOVE when redundancy/uselessness is established and useful material and consumers survive.
- DEFER for a named missing fact or decision that materially prevents a verdict; identify its owner
  and consequence. Ordinary source discovery is the agent's work, not a default question to offload.

Keep intent, implementation, procedure and evidence distinguishable. Supersede historical evidence
with new dated observations instead of rewriting it. Generated/executable owners carry mutable
inventories; authored guidance explains meaning, prerequisites and decisions. Scope consumer searches
to repository links, source references and known callers; zero inbound links alone proves little.

Independent investigation is useful only for a concrete judgment gap and when delegation is authorized.
Group questions sharing source context. Give a realistic question, raw evidence, permitted actions
and a stopping boundary; avoid supplying the desired answer for an independent trial. The primary
owns semantic synthesis and consequential decisions; subagent output is evidence. Use actual supported
roles and project routing rather than embedding a model mandate in a documentation method.

Report why the route is usable, what uncertainty remains and why more work would or would not change
the disposition. Local structural checks cannot establish production readiness or provider eligibility.
