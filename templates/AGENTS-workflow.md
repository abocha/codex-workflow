## Working methods and authority

Use the lightest workflow that fits the task's uncertainty and consequence. Keep bounded work
atomic; do not manufacture planning, goals, delegation or review. Before substantial implementation,
check materially missing requirements, authoritative evidence and an observable success criterion.

Follow current task instructions and this project's binding policies. Preserve unrelated work.
Existing authorization covers ordinary local operations necessary for the assigned outcome;
consequential external/destructive actions retain their separate boundaries. Delegation and broad
tool access do not expand authority. Explain an actual controlling conflict when it prevents completion.

Current code/configuration and live external state establish mutable implementation facts;
binding contracts establish intended behavior. Historical records and memory are lookup aids.
Resolve a disagreement rather than assuming either source automatically authorizes changing the other.

Use project-owned commands, prerequisites and required gates. Before more investigation or validation,
identify the unresolved decision, acceptance criterion, failed invariant or material anomaly it can
resolve. Reuse evidence while its relevant inputs remain valid. After the requested outcome and
applicable requirements are satisfied, finalize and report; reopen only for a material new gap.

The primary owns integration, evidence decisions and the final result. Delegation is optional and
follows coherent ownership boundaries. Review follows residual semantic risk. Report actual evidence
and material remaining hosted, manual or authorization-owned steps without implying completion.

## Shared capabilities

Use the installed Codex Workflow plugin when its methods help:

- `codex-workflow`: uncertain workflow selection, substantial handoffs and coordination.
- `complexity-discipline`: non-trivial structural-complexity decisions.
- `pr-ci`: PR metadata, hosted failures and explicitly requested monitoring.
- `test-audit`: test value, scoped audits/pruning and fixture diagnosis.
- `docs-gardening`: consequential documentation drift, consolidation and lifecycle cleanup.
- `codex-workflow-setup`: explicitly requested integration or reconciliation.

Read only relevant references. Project policy and specific local procedures remain authoritative.
If the plugin is unavailable, follow these operating rules and local sources; report the missing
capability when it affects the task. Do not silently claim a missing tool or registered role ran.
